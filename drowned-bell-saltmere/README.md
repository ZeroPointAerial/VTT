# The Drowned Bell of Saltmere

A D&D 5e (2014 rules) one-shot for four to five level 5 characters, packaged as a Foundry VTT module.

**Requires:** Foundry VTT v13 or v14, and the dnd5e system (5.0 or later).

## Install

1. Unzip so the folder `drowned-bell-saltmere` sits inside your Foundry user data at `Data/modules/`.
   The result should be `Data/modules/drowned-bell-saltmere/module.json`.
2. Restart Foundry (or return to Setup), launch your dnd5e world, and enable **The Drowned Bell of Saltmere** under *Manage Modules*.
3. In the Compendium sidebar, open **The Drowned Bell of Saltmere (Adventure)** and click **Import Adventure**.
4. Open the journal **The Drowned Bell of Saltmere** and read *Running It in Foundry*.

## Contents

- **Scenes:** Saltmere & the Grey Cove (parchment overview), Saltmere Village at low tide (night-ready), the Tidal Caves, the Drowned Chapel of St. Corwen. All with walls, doors, windows, lights, ambient sound and journal map pins.
- **Actors:** Mother Silt, Drowned Thrall, Giant Octopus, Merrow, Father Anselm's ghost, the Bell of St. Corwen (targetable object), six named villagers, the seven captives, and a generic Sleepwalking Villager.
- **Items:** Bellshard of St. Corwen, Charm of Restoration, Pearl of Power, Cloak of the Manta Ray, Potion of Water Breathing, and the clue items.
- **Journals:** the full adventure (22 pages), Saltmere Locations, Characters of Saltmere, and six image handouts (including a genuinely solvable cipher).
- **Roll tables:** Saltmere Gossip, Who Hears the Bell?, Tidal Cave Complications, Thrall Remains, Mother Silt's Whispers.
- **Audio:** drowned bell toll, true bell toll, surf, cave drips, chapel drone, hag's whispers.
- **Saltmere Tracker:** an on-screen panel for Tide Marks, True Tolls and Villagers Saved; automatically reveals the flood tiles in the caves and the golden light in the chapel. Macros: The Bell Tolls, Ring the Bell True, Spend Tide Marks, Father Anselm Appears, Saltmere Tracker, Reset the Night.

All maps, tokens, handouts and sounds are original and were generated for this module. Monster stat blocks for the giant octopus and merrow, and the SRD magic items, come from the dnd5e system's SRD 5.1 content (CC-BY-4.0). Fonts used in the artwork: IM Fell, Cinzel, Pirata One and Homemade Apple (SIL Open Font License / Apache).
